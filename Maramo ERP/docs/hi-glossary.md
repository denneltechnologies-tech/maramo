# Classivo English to Hindi (hi) glossary

Standard terms for every `lang/hi` file. Simple Hindi as used in Indian schools and CBSE circulars. Keep placeholders (`:name`) exactly as in English. Keep acronyms (GPA, UDISE+, EPF, ESI, IFSC, PDF, CSV, QR) in Latin or common Devanagari form.
