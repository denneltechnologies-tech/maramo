# Code of Conduct

We want Classivo to be a friendly place to work on school software. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, insults or discrimination.
- Never share other people's private information, including student data.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block people who break these rules. To report a problem, email **info@classivoerp.com**.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
